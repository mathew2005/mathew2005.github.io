Starting Small, Learning Fast

One of the best ways to grow as a developer is to begin with small projects and keep improving them over time. Simple ideas often become the foundation for bigger and better work.

I enjoy building things that are practical, clean, and easy to understand. The process of designing, coding, and refining an idea is where a lot of learning happens.

My goal is to keep learning, stay curious, and build experiences that people can use and enjoy.
